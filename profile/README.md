## About GSLHub

**GSLHub** is an independent research initiative and open technological infrastructure focused on understanding how generative AI systems discover, select, cite and recommend information.

Our work combines **Generative Search research, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI evaluation, governed evidence, reproducible experimentation and software engineering**.

The goal is not only to study generative systems, but to build the infrastructure required to make that research **auditable, repeatable and useful in real-world environments**.

## Research focus

GSLHub currently focuses on:

- **Generative Search** — how AI systems retrieve, synthesize and surface information.
- **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** — factors that influence whether sources are selected, cited or recommended by generative systems.
- **AI system evaluation** — controlled comparison of model and interface behavior.
- **Evidence governance** — linking prompts, executions, observations, evidence and preserved research artifacts.
- **Reproducible research** — preserving execution context, methodology and research outputs so results can be independently reviewed.
- **Open technological innovation** — turning research infrastructure, metrics and software into reusable public resources.

## Open research infrastructure

Our main public repository is [`gslhub/website`](https://github.com/gslhub/website), which contains the GSLHub research platform and its public website.

The platform includes infrastructure for:

- research projects and experiments;
- prompt execution and execution-environment tracking;
- evidence and research-artifact provenance;
- scientific metrics and validation workflows;
- datasets, publications, benchmarks and software outputs;
- administrative research operations and reproducibility controls.

## Repository structure

GSLHub is being organized around several research and engineering streams:

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **website** | Public site and core research platform |
| **research** | Research protocols, experiments and study materials |
| **datasets** | Curated and documented research datasets |
| **benchmarks** | Evaluation frameworks and reproducible benchmarks |
| **software** | Reusable research software and technical tools |
| **docs** | Technical and methodological documentation |
| **branding** | Brand guidelines and approved visual assets |

Repositories are opened progressively when their contents are ready for public reuse and documentation.

## Principles

We aim to build research that is:

**Transparent** — methodology and technical decisions should be inspectable.  
**Reproducible** — experiments should preserve enough context to be repeated.  
**Evidence-driven** — conclusions should remain traceable to preserved evidence.  
**Technically rigorous** — research infrastructure should be engineered with the same care as production software.  
**Open where possible** — software, methods and outputs should be reusable whenever legal, ethical and methodological constraints allow it.
